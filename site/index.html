Coffee story

A double anaerobic natural from Ximeng in Yunnan, processed by Torch Coffee. Red currant up front, a rum-like sweetness in the finish.

Brew guide

Pour over 1:16 at 94°C, 3 minutes. AeroPress 15 g to 230 g, 2 minutes. French press 1:15, 4 minutes.
